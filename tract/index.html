What Tract is

Tract is a self-tracking wellness app. Log water and bathroom visits, review history, run bladder-training and Kegel routines, and search for nearby toilets when you need one.

It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Training features are educational. Talk to a qualified clinician before starting a program if you have a medical concern.

Find Toilet uses third-party and crowd-sourced map data. Pins can be wrong or closed. Do not rely on it as the only option in an emergency — verify on the ground.

What’s free, and Tract+

Tracking, history, and Find Toilet stay free.

Tract+ is an optional Apple subscription for training features: $0.99/month or $9.99/year. Payment is handled by Apple. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Privacy by default

  • Health logs stay on your device (SwiftData). No Tract account.
  • Find Toilet sends precise location only for the search you request.
  • Optional anonymous device fingerprint for toilet-feedback rate limiting — not tied to your Apple ID.
  • No ads and no analytics SDKs.
