Tract — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 May 2026

Summary

Tract is designed to be private by default. All of your hydration, bathroom, bladder-training, and Kegel data is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. We do not run servers that hold your health information, we do not ship a third-party analytics SDK, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Data we collect

  • Health-related logs (water intake, bathroom visits, bladder-training schedules, Kegel sessions, urge events, streaks) — stored locally on your device only.
  • App preferences (daily water goal, reminder settings) — stored locally on your device in UserDefaults.
  • Approximate location — used only when you tap “Find Nearby Toilets” to query the OpenStreetMap Overpass API. Your location is never stored, logged, or transmitted to us.

Data we don't collect

  • We do not run accounts or sign-in.
  • We do not collect crash reports, usage analytics, or device identifiers.
  • We do not embed third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Subscriptions

Tract+ subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple via StoreKit. Apple sends Tract only the receipt necessary to unlock paid features on your device. Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction.

Third-party services

  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API — receives your approximate coordinates only when you actively request nearby toilets. Governed by the OSM Foundation policies.
  • Apple StoreKit — handles subscription billing as described above.

Your control

You can delete every piece of data Tract holds at any time from Settings → Delete All Data. Uninstalling the app removes all local data as well.

Children

Tract is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the app indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
